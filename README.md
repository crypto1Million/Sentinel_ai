# Sentinel AI

Sentinel AI is a Solana memecoin intelligence and trading terminal designed to provide traders with institutional-grade analytics, wallet intelligence, narrative tracking, risk analysis, and AI-powered scoring.

## Core Features

### Trading Terminal

* TradingView Charts
* Jupiter Swaps
* Raydium Integration
* Real-time Market Data
* Portfolio Tracking

### Sentinel Score

* Dev Quality
* Wallet Quality
* Volume Quality
* Social Strength
* Narrative Momentum
* Rug Risk
* Opportunity Score

### Wallet DNA

* Smart Money Detection
* Wallet Reputation
* Wallet Specialization
* Wallet Copy Score
* Funding Graph Analysis

### Rug Radar

* Bundle Detection
* Sniper Detection
* Insider Detection
* Mint Authority Check
* Freeze Authority Check
* LP Analysis

### Narrative Engine

* AI Meta Tracking
* Animal Meta Tracking
* Political Meta Tracking
* Gaming Meta Tracking
* Celebrity Meta Tracking

### J7 Intelligence

* Signal Detection
* Trending Narratives
* Wallet Mentions
* Token Mentions
* Market Intelligence
